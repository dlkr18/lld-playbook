The one idea to carry the whole way

Under all the jargon, there are just two engines: a generative model (text → next text, to produce) and an embedding model (text → numbers, to compare). RAG, agents, tools, fine-tuning, vector DBs — all of it is plumbing and orchestration around those two. Hold that and nothing here will feel like magic.

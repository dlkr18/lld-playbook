12.2 Reliability engineering

LLM APIs rate-limit, time out, and occasionally return garbage. Agents compound small errors: a 2% failure rate per step is an 18% failure rate over a 10-step trajectory (0.98¹⁰ ≈ 0.82). Engineer for it — this chapter is the module you drop into the thin harness from the build-vs-buy chapter.

Handbook

12.3 Evaluation

Agents are non-deterministic; a prompt tweak can shift a whole trajectory. Evals are how you know a change helped, not hurt. This chapter builds the complete harness: a dataset format, a runner, deterministic and judge graders, a CI gate, and the statistics that stop you from shipping noise. Three levels of eval, then the machinery.

Handbook

12.4 Observability

You cannot debug a non-deterministic, multi-step, tool-calling system from print statements. Trace everything — with OpenTelemetry-shaped spans so the data outlives your current vendor — then aggregate the spans into metrics with alert thresholds you've actually chosen.
